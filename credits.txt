The Block Survival — Third-Party Resource Pack Credits
======================================================

Vanilla Tweaks  (https://vanillatweaks.net)
-------------------------------------------
This modpack bundles a curated Vanilla Tweaks resource pack
(resourcepacks/VanillaTweaks.zip), generated from vanillatweaks.net for
Minecraft 26.2 and shipped as part of The Block Survival's larger resource-pack
stack (Fresh Animations, Patrix, Complementary shaders) and mod configuration.

Included Vanilla Tweaks packs:
  Clearer Water, Lower Fire, Lower Shield, Borderless Glass, Variated Birch Logs,
  Variated Bookshelves, Variated Cobblestone, Variated Planks, Ore Borders,
  Brighter Nether.

All Vanilla Tweaks content is (c) Vanilla Tweaks and its contributors, used under
the Vanilla Tweaks Terms (https://vanillatweaks.net/terms/). The Block Survival is
free to download and play. Please support Vanilla Tweaks at https://vanillatweaks.net.
